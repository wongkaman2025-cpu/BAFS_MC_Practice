📌 導言 Introduction

本解析指南已嚴謹對照教育局最新發布的《2025 年及以後文憑試最新課程補充資料（20251230）》與《歷年試題趨勢分析》。為了協助您完全聚焦於最新制卷一（必修部分）的考核範圍，所有已從最新必修考綱中完全刪除、或自 2025 年起已被歸入卷二（選修會計單元 Paper 2A）的題目，均已被明確標註為 [out c]。

This bilingual guide is fully aligned with the latest curriculum guidelines for the 2025 HKDSE and beyond. For candidates preparing for Paper 1 (Compulsory Part), all questions that are either 'completely removed' or 'transferred to Paper 2A (Accounting Elective Module)' under the new syllabus are clearly marked as [out c] to ensure maximum revision efficiency.
