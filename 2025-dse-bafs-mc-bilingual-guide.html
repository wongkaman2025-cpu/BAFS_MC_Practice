📌 導言 Introduction

本解析指南依據最新 2025 DSE 課程編製。自 2025 年起，BAFS 卷一必修部分不再包含任何基礎會計與會計等式記帳等純計算與分錄考點，相關會計實務已全數移撥至卷二會計選修模組（Paper 2A）。本指南嚴格收錄了 2025 年最新 Paper 1A 中的 30 道精華選擇題。

This bilingual guide is fully aligned with the 2025 HKDSE curriculum. Starting from 2025, Paper 1 Compulsory section no longer includes introductory accounting and bookkeeping practices, which have been moved to Paper 2A. This guide收录了 30 MC questions from the 2025 Paper 1A.
